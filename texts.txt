3. STEP 1 — "What are you adding?"
================================================================================

  📋  "One-time task"
      "Something to do, no particular time"
        does: no date at all. Always counts as Actual, because it has no
              position in time to be early or late for.

  📅  "Event"
      "Happens at a specific date and time"
        does: one moment. Moves Upcoming -> Actual -> Passed around that moment.

  🔁  "Repeats"
      "A rule that keeps making events for you"
        does: not a task. A rule. It keeps exactly one event waiting in your
              list, and makes the next one when that is done or gone by.

6. STEP 3 — the timing block  (event, daily, weekly, monthly — not one-time)
================================================================================

This is the part you flagged. All three are DURATIONS measured from the task's
own moment, not clock times.

  "NOTIFY BEFORE"  [ 0 ]   "BEFORE"  [ minutes ▾ ]
        units offered: minutes / hours / days
        does: how long before the task's date the push notification is sent.
              0 = the notification arrives at the date itself.

  Collapsed section, tap to open:
    "When it shows in the list"

      "MOVE TO ACTUALS BEFORE"  [ 1 ]   "BEFORE"  [ days ▾ ]
            does: how long before the date the task moves out of Upcoming
                  and into Actual. Default 1 day.

      "STAY ACTIVE AFTER"  [ 10 ]   "THE DATE, BY"  [ minutes ▾ ]
            does: how long after the date it stays in Actual before it
                  becomes Passed. Default 10 minutes. Counted from the
                  date itself — confirmed in the code.

      "All three are counted from the task's own date. A notification cannot
       arrive before the task appears, so "notify before" is capped at "move
       to actuals before"."
            does: real rule, enforced by the server. The form silently clamps
                  "notify before" down rather than showing an error.

  Still open while you reword: the three labels have to make sense against the
  three filter buttons at the top of the list, named "Upcoming", "Actual" and
  "Passed". "Move to actuals before" now says one of them out loud; the other
  two fields still do not.

7. STEP 3 — fields that appear only for certain types
================================================================================

  EVENT only:
    "DATE"                                         (date + time picker)

  DAILY, WEEKLY and MONTHLY — the same control for all three now:
    "TIMES"
      [ Set times ] [ Every N hours ]              two ways of entering them

      "Set times" mode:
        rows of [ 09:00 ] with "✕", and "+ Time"

      "Every N hours" mode:
        "FROM"    [ 09:00 ]
        "UNTIL"   [ 23:00 ]
        "EVERY"   [ 02:00 ]
        hint: "Saved as the times this works out to, so you can adjust them
               one by one afterwards."

        does: both modes end up as a plain list of times. "Every" is a gap,
              not a clock time — 02:00 means every 2 hours. Only the list is
              stored, so reopening for an edit always shows "Set times".

  DAILY and WEEKLY:
    "DAYS"      Mo Tu We Th Fr Sa Su
        does: daily arrives with all seven on, weekly starts on Monday alone.

  MONTHLY only:
    "DAY OF MONTH"  [ 1 ]
    "MONTHS"        Jan Feb Mar Apr May Jun Jul Aug Sep Oct Nov Dec

9. THINGS THE FORM SAYS BACK TO YOU
================================================================================

These replace the step-count line under the title when a submit is refused.

  "Pick at least one day — with none, this would never come round."
        when: you untick every weekday.

  "Add at least one time — with none, this would never come round."
        when: you clear every time row in "Set times" mode.

  "That window produces no times. Check that "until" is after "from" and
   "every" is not zero."
        when: "Every N hours" mode is given a window that yields nothing.

        note: the old "crosses midnight UTC" refusal is gone. Discrete times
              have no window to wrap, so 21:00 and 01:00 are simply two
              entries — though "Every N hours" mode still cannot express it.

  "That photo would not load. Check the link on the task."           (new)
        when: the photo popup gets a url the browser cannot fetch.

10. THE REPEATS LIST  (Options ⌄ -> "Repeats")
================================================================================

  "Repeats"
  "Each one keeps a single event waiting in the list. Deleting a repeat
   removes the events it made."

  Each row reads:   <category icon> <name> — <schedule>
    daily:    "09:00, 11:00, 13:00 · Mo Tu We Th Fr"
              or "8 a day, 09:00–23:00" once there are more than four
    weekly:   "07:00, 18:00 · Mo Th"
    monthly:  "day 5 of Sep Oct, 12:00"

    note: the raw weekday and month numbers are gone — days and months now
          print as names, and every row shows the times it fires at.
